# Research Highlight — Brand System

## Brand idea

Research Highlight sits between two familiar research actions:

- reading and marking what matters;
- turning those marks into structured knowledge that can be retrieved later.

The visual system therefore combines a **highlighted document** with a **small structured-data motif**.

## Primary mark

Canonical asset:

`assets/brand/research-highlight-icon.svg`

The approved direction is intentionally compact: one document, one highlighted passage, and one minimal chart. Avoid adding decorative AI sparkles, molecular structures, multiple charts, or excessive interface chrome to the core mark.

## Color system

Primary palette:

- Deep indigo: `#24213F`
- Indigo: `#3A356F`
- Violet: `#6257A8`
- Highlight amber: `#FFB000`
- Highlight soft amber: `#FFD161`
- Paper: `#FFFFFF`
- Secondary neutral: `#A7A8BB`

The amber highlight is the strongest brand accent. Indigo carries the research/software identity.

## Product naming

Umbrella product:

**Research Highlight**

Current modules:

- **Research Highlight AI** — Zotero
- **Research Highlight Dashboard** — Obsidian

Do not present the two plugins as unrelated utilities. They are two surfaces of the same product.

## Voice

Product copy should be concise, technical, and research-oriented.

Prefer:

- structured research knowledge
- reusable evidence
- retrieval across papers
- local-first
- provider-flexible
- Zotero-native
- research workflow

Avoid:

- personal hack
- little tool
- my workflow
- script for myself
- magic AI notes
- second brain hype

## Tagline

Primary:

> **Turn literature highlights into reusable research knowledge.**

Short form:

> **From highlights to research knowledge.**

## Icon use across clients

The same brand mark should be used for both Zotero and Obsidian packaging.

Client-specific UI icons may use simplified monochrome variants when the host application requires a single-color toolbar glyph, but they should preserve the same document + highlight silhouette.
